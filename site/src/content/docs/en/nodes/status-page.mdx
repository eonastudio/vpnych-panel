---
title: Status page
description: A public page — whether the servers work right now and their daily uptime.
---

A public page where clients see for themselves whether the servers work and how they worked over the last days. It opens at `<subscription address>/status`, for example `https://sub.example.com/status`.

## Turning it on

The **Status page** card in **Settings → Subscription**:

- **Show the page**: while it is off, the address returns 404.
- **Title**: up to 80 characters. Empty means "Server status" in the visitor's language.
- **Days to show**: 30, 60 or 90, 90 by default.

The card has a preview and the "Open" and "Copy address" buttons.

## Which servers are on the page

Only enabled nodes with a **public name**. It is set in the node card, in the "Name in status channel" field, for example `🇩🇪 Germany`. Visitors see this name: addresses, IPs and internal node names do not reach the page. A node without a public name is not shown.

## What the visitor sees

At the top, a summary: all servers are up, some are down, or all are down. Below it, for each server:

- the state right now: "Up", "Down" or "No data";
- the uptime over the chosen period, in percent;
- a bar per day: the day's color shows the share of checks the node passed. A day without checks is marked "no data".

The page is in Russian or English by the browser's language; if the browser does not say, in the panel's language. With `?format=json` the same address returns JSON for your own widgets and monitoring.

## Where the uptime comes from

The panel already checks its connection to the nodes regularly. Since this version it counts, for each day (UTC), how many checks there were and how many the node passed: that share is the day's uptime. The counters are written to the database about once a minute, and the days are kept for 100 days.

## Limits

- The history starts with the update: days before it show as "no data".
- Uptime is the panel's connection to the node, not reachability from a client's network. If only the clients of some ISP are blocked, the page will not see it.
- While the panel is down there are no checks, and those hours do not count.
- The data on the page refreshes at most once every 30 seconds.
- Anyone who knows the address can open the page: do not give nodes public names you do not want to show.
